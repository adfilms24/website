LOCO BIRRIÁ — RESTAURANT CONTENT

Video and social media content for the restaurant LOCO BIRRIÁ in Zurich: kitchen, dishes, bar and team, produced for Instagram, TikTok and the website.

Challenge

LOCO BIRRIÁ needed regular content for Instagram and TikTok that shows what guests experience on site: the kitchen, the dishes and the bar – without a studio look.

What we did

One shooting day on site: slow-motion food shots, the kitchen and team in action, the bar and atmosphere. The result: vertical reels, 4:5 clips with subtitles and a follow call-to-action, and short videos in which the team presents the menu themselves.

Result

The team is happy and the collaboration continues: we deliver content for the coming weeks, currently also for their time at Legere until December.
