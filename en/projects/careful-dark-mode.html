CAREFUL DARK MODE CLUB BELLEVUE

Aftermovie of the Halloween party "Dark Mode" by party label Careful on 30 October 2026 at Club Bellevue Zurich: DJs, costumes and a crowd dancing until the end.

Challenge

On 30 October 2026, party label Careful hosted its Halloween special "Dark Mode" at Club Bellevue in Zurich. The goal was an aftermovie that makes people want to come to the next party.

What we did

We were right on the dance floor: neon light, costumes, DJs on the decks and the crowd up close. Cut to the beat, with a pink and black title look matching the flyer.

Result

Alongside the 16:9 film for the website, we delivered a vertical version for Instagram.
