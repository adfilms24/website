SUSHI RESTAURANT ZURICH

Film for the opening of a sushi restaurant in Zurich: food tasting, the chef at work, knives, fish and craft in calm, documentary images.

Challenge

For the opening, the restaurant wanted to show what guests can expect: fresh fish, real craft and a chef who knows what he is doing.

What we did

We covered the opening food tasting and portrayed the chef in the kitchen. The result was a landscape film for the website and vertical versions for social media.
