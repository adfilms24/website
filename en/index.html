About us

YOUR VIDEO,
no detours

We are A.D.Films24 Studio, a video studio in Zurich. We produce videos for SMEs and companies — from brand films and social media content to events.

Our clients get what was agreed, and usually a bit more. So far, not a single revision request. Because we settle what the video needs to achieve before the shoot, and then deliver exactly that.

Transparent from the start: a clear process, a clear price and an honest assessment, even when something doesn't fit.
