Our clients get what was agreed, and usually a bit more. So far, not a single revision request. Because we settle what the video needs to achieve before the shoot, and then deliver exactly that.

FAQ

FREQUENT
questions

What does a video with A.D.Films24 cost?

It mainly depends on the number of shooting days, locations and versions. After a short call you get a clear quote with a fixed price – no hidden costs.

How long does it take to get the finished video?

Depending on the scope, a few days to a few weeks. We set the exact timeline together in the quote.

How many revision rounds are needed?

We settle exactly what the video needs to achieve before the shoot. That's why there hasn't been a single revision request so far. And if something doesn't fit, we find a solution.

Do you also shoot outside Zurich?

Yes. We work all over Switzerland and internationally.

Which formats do we get?

Whatever you need: for example 16:9 for website and YouTube, 9:16 for reels and TikTok, 4:5 for the feed.

Do you offer drone footage?

Yes, on request – with an A1/A3 drone licence for Switzerland.
